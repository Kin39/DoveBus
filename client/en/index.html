Data freshness

Every dot on the map has an age, and it is written next to it.

A position is not “now” just because it has only just appeared on screen. DoveBus counts the seconds since the last data received from each vehicle and always shows them. This is the real scale the product uses, not an illustration.

The live trip

When you follow a trip, you see everything that can be known.

The whole route
The road the vehicle has to take, terminus to terminus, as it stands in the official timetable — not a straight line between two points.
The stretch already travelled
Reconstructed along the real road. The GPS positions received fall on the published route within a few metres, so the trail follows the bends instead of cutting through the hills.
Late, early, cancelled
On time, +7 min, or cancelled when the operator says the trip is not running. Roughly nine trips under way out of ten carry this figure.
How full it is
From “empty” to “standing only”, when the vehicle declares it. Many do not, and in that case nothing is written.
How much we are guessing
Between one GPS fix and the next the vehicle is advanced along its route, so that it is not left standing for two minutes. A circle shows how far off that estimate could be, and the positions genuinely received stay drawn separately: the reconstruction is never confused with the observation.

Said before you find out on your own

What DoveBus is not.

It is not an official service

DoveBus is an independent project. It is neither run nor endorsed by Autolinee Toscane or Regione Toscana: it reads the open data that is published, and that is all. For official information about tickets, timetables and disruptions, the operator remains the place to go.

It does not see people

It sees vehicles. It does not know who is on board, it locates nobody and it never asks where you are: DoveBus does not use the browser's geolocation. The privacy page explains it in full.

It does not know about trips added at the last minute

About 3% of the trips on the road do not exist in the day's official timetable: they are services the operator added. They still appear on the map, but with fewer details — the planned route may be missing.

It does not invent a position it does not have

If a vehicle has not reported for more than five minutes it disappears from the map, and the estimate of where it would have got to stops being extended. A stationary dot that looks current is worse than no dot.

It does not plan your journey

It does not work out itineraries with changes, does not compare alternatives and does not sell tickets. It does one thing: it says where the vehicles are, now.

Frequently asked

The things worth knowing.

What is DoveBus?

An independent web map that shows where the buses in Tuscany are, using the open data published by Regione Toscana. It opens in a browser, it is not an app and it has no account.

Which lines does it cover?

The Autolinee Toscane lines present in the regional feed: around 610 town and intercity lines, touching 267 Tuscan municipalities and almost 29,000 stops. Florence and Siena alone account for about a hundred each; smaller towns often have one or two. In Florence this includes the town lines that ATAF ran until 2021.

Do I have to install an app or sign up?

No. DoveBus works from the browser of a phone, a tablet or a computer. There is no account, no email address to leave and nothing to install — though a phone will let you add it to the home screen, if you want.

How up to date are the positions?

Regione Toscana's feed regenerates about every 120 seconds and DoveBus re-reads it every 30 seconds, so a position arrives already about two minutes old. That is why the age of the data is always written next to the vehicle instead of being hidden behind the word “live”. The full path of the data is here.

Why is a bus missing from the map?

Three possible reasons: it is not in service at that moment, it is not transmitting its position, or its last position is more than five minutes old. In that last case DoveBus removes it from the map on purpose: a vehicle drawn where it was five minutes ago is wrong information dressed up as information.

Can I follow a person who is on the bus?

No. DoveBus sees vehicles, not people: it does not know who is on board, it has no way of knowing, and it never asks for the position of whoever is using it.

Is DoveBus an official Autolinee Toscane service?

No. It is an independent, unofficial project, not affiliated with Autolinee Toscane or with Regione Toscana. All it does is read the open data that is published and draw it on a map, as the CC BY 4.0 licence expressly allows. Every source and licence is listed here.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. There are no subscriptions, no advertising and no profiling cookies. The site only counts how often each page is opened, on a server of its own: the privacy page says exactly what that records.
